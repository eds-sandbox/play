# Play

Small browser games and experiments. Each game is a single `index.html` with no dependencies or build step: open it in a browser, or play them all on the site:

**https://eds-sandbox.github.io/play/**

| Game | What it is |
|---|---|
| [Shadow Match](https://eds-sandbox.github.io/play/shadow-match/) | Turn a 3D shape until the shadow it casts on the wall matches the target outline. |
| [Tiny Ant Colony](https://eds-sandbox.github.io/play/tiny-ant-colony/) | Ants find food by following each other's scent trails. Place food and walls and watch the paths form. |
| [Light Bender](https://eds-sandbox.github.io/play/light-bender/) | Turn and place mirrors and splitters so every ring gets exactly the colour of light it needs. |
| [Gravity Doodle](https://eds-sandbox.github.io/play/gravity-doodle/) | Draw lines with limited ink so the falling balls bounce and roll into the cup. |
| [Grapple Swing](https://eds-sandbox.github.io/play/grapple-swing/) | Hold to grab a ring and reel in, let go to fly. Get as far as you can over the lava. |
| [Orbit Golf](https://eds-sandbox.github.io/play/orbit-golf/) | Mini golf in space: one shot per try, and every planet bends the ball's path with its gravity. |
| [Jelly Stack](https://eds-sandbox.github.io/play/jelly-stack/) | Drop wobbly jelly blocks onto a tower that sways more the higher it gets. |
| [Ice Slide](https://eds-sandbox.github.io/play/ice-slide/) | A sliding puzzle on ice: you only stop when you hit a rock. Every level has exactly one shortest route. |
| [Pipe Flow](https://eds-sandbox.github.io/play/pipe-flow/) | Place pipes from the queue before the water reaches them. Each round the water comes sooner and flows faster. |
| [Echo Dash](https://eds-sandbox.github.io/play/echo-dash/) | Grab the orb, but every run you make comes back as a ghost. Dodge all your past selves. |
| [Lighthouse](https://eds-sandbox.github.io/play/lighthouse/) | One button: hold to sweep the beam. Ships you light up can see the rocks and steer around them. |
| [Typing Defense](https://eds-sandbox.github.io/play/typing-defense/) | Words march on your base. Type a word to lock on and fire; waves get bigger and the words longer. |
| [Shrink Snake](https://eds-sandbox.github.io/play/shrink-snake/) | Snake in reverse: you start long and every bite makes you shorter. Get down to length 1 before your tail grows back. |
| [Hex Territory](https://eds-sandbox.github.io/play/hex-territory/) | Claim hexes and surround enemy groups to flip them to your colour. Play a friend or a minimax AI. |
| [Market Stall](https://eds-sandbox.github.io/play/market-stall/) | Run a juice stall for a 14-day season: read the weather, buy supplies, set your recipe and price. |
| [Sound Maze](https://eds-sandbox.github.io/play/sound-maze/) | Find the exit of a dark maze by ear: follow the beacon's beeps and use echoes to hear which ways are open. |
| [Block Saboteur](https://eds-sandbox.github.io/play/block-saboteur/) | Reverse falling blocks: you pick the pieces, a smart AI places them. Make it overflow in as few pieces as you can. |
| [Dungeon](https://eds-sandbox.github.io/play/dungeon/) | A small roguelike with a terminal look: explore generated floors, fight monsters, find stairs and go deeper. |

Every game is in its own folder (`<name>/index.html`). Ideas for more are in [`idea.md`](idea.md).
