Play

Small browser games and experiments.

Shadow Match →

Turn a 3D shape until the shadow it casts on the wall matches the target outline.

Tiny Ant Colony →

Ants find food by following each other's scent trails. Place food and walls and watch the paths form.

Light Bender →

Turn and place mirrors and splitters so every ring gets exactly the colour of light it needs.

Gravity Doodle →

Draw lines with limited ink so the falling balls bounce and roll into the cup.

Grapple Swing →

Hold to grab a ring and reel in, let go to fly. Get as far as you can over the lava.

Orbit Golf →

Mini golf in space: one shot per try, and every planet bends the ball's path with its gravity.

Jelly Stack →

Drop wobbly jelly blocks onto a tower that sways more the higher it gets.

Ice Slide →

A sliding puzzle on ice: you only stop when you hit a rock. Every level has exactly one shortest route.

Pipe Flow →

Place pipes from the queue before the water reaches them. Each round the water comes sooner and flows faster.

Echo Dash →

Grab the orb, but every run you make comes back as a ghost. Dodge all your past selves.

Lighthouse →

One button: hold to sweep the beam. Ships you light up can see the rocks and steer around them.

Typing Defense →

Words march on your base. Type a word to lock on and fire; waves get bigger and the words longer.

Shrink Snake →

Snake in reverse: you start long and every bite makes you shorter. Get down to length 1 before your tail grows back.

Hex Territory →

Claim hexes and surround enemy groups to flip them to your colour. Play a friend or a minimax AI.

Market Stall →

Run a juice stall for a 14-day season: read the weather, buy supplies, set your recipe and price.

Sound Maze →

Find the exit of a dark maze by ear: follow the beacon's beeps and use echoes to hear which ways are open.

Block Saboteur →

Reverse falling blocks: you pick the pieces, a smart AI places them. Make it overflow in as few pieces as you can.

Dungeon →

A small roguelike with a terminal look: explore generated floors, fight monsters, find stairs and go deeper.
