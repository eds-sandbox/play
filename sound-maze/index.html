Sound Maze

You're in a dark maze. The exit beeps: it's to your left or right depending on which ear you hear it in, higher-pitched when it's above you, and it beeps faster as your path to it gets shorter.

Move with arrow keys / WASD or swipe. Press Space for an echo: a bright tick means open, a dull knock means wall (left ear, above, right ear, below). Can't use sound? Turn on Visual mode.
